{%- comment -%} Opens a verse block inside any post (e.g. lyrics on an audio post). Close it with end_verse.html. Line breaks, blank lines (stanzas) and leading spaces are kept exactly: {% include verse.html %} The grill was cold that morning, the cheese still in its paper. {% include end_verse.html %} Param: align — optional "left" to left-set the block (default: centered) Posts with `format: verse` don't need this; their whole body is verse. The lines themselves are wrapped by verse-lines.html, from post.html. {%- endcomment -%}
