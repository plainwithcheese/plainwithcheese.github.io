{%- comment -%} Estimated reading time, under a post's dek: "Serving: est. 12 mins", or "Serving: est. 1 min" (wording in _data/diner.yml, `serving`). Shown only when the post's section has `reading_time: true` (_data/categories.yml: Burgers and Drinks) and its format is listed under `reading_time` in _data/formats.yml (text). Worked out at build time, so there's no JavaScript: the body's words (tags stripped) divided by `reading_wpm` in _config.yml (230), rounded to the nearest whole minute, at least 1. Param: html — the post's rendered body (the layout passes `content`) {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign rt_section = site.data.categories | where: "slug", page.category | first -%} {%- if rt_section.reading_time == true and site.data.formats.reading_time contains page.format -%} {%- assign rt_words = include.html | strip_html | number_of_words -%} {%- assign rt_rate = site.reading_wpm | default: 230 | times: 1.0 -%} {%- assign rt_mins = rt_words | divided_by: rt_rate | round -%} {%- if rt_mins < 1 -%}{%- assign rt_mins = 1 -%}{%- endif -%} {%- assign rt_text = site.data.diner.serving %}
