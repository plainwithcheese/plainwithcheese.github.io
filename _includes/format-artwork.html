{%- comment -%} `format: artwork` — one painting, drawing or sketch, shown as the piece. The lead image is large, and a click enlarges it (the gallery's lightbox, assets/js/lightbox.js). Under it: its caption and the work's details line. Any further images of the same work (details, angles, process shots) follow as small thumbnails, which open in the same lightbox. The post body follows as a note. For a set of different works, use `gallery`. Front matter: images — one or more; the first is the lead. Each needs `src` and `alt`; `caption` is optional (60 characters or fewer). `src` follows media-url.html: "/…" is a file in the repo, and a path without the "/" is on the media host. There, each also needs `width` and `height` (the full-size file's pixels; the build can't measure it), and `widths` lists its smaller copies, e.g. [800, 1200] for jellyfish-800.jpg and jellyfish-1200.jpg beside jellyfish.jpg. Checked in validate-format-content.html. medium — optional, e.g. "Watercolour on paper" dimensions — optional, e.g. "9 × 12 in." (the multiplication sign ×, not the letter x) year — optional: the year the work was made, as free text ("c. 2019", "2019–21"). Without it, the year of `created:` is used. Never filled in from the post's date or a photo's. The details line (the work line, artwork-details.html) shows whichever of the three are set, joined by " · ", and is left out with none. Cards and menu rows show it in place of the dek when there's no dek. There are no figure numbers. The lead image: its `width` and `height` reserve its space before it loads, and with `widths` the browser picks the smallest copy that fills the column at the screen's density (`sizes`: the column is 592px, or the window less main's 24px gutters on phones). It's fetched first (fetchpriority), being the page's largest picture. WebP: an image on the media host is served as WebP where the browser takes it (a with the same widths), with its JPG (or PNG) as the fallback; the lightbox opens the WebP too. The files sit beside the originals with the same names (jellyfish-800.webp). An image without them sets `webp: false`. Share cards and the feed keep the originals. The card thumbnail is the lead image unless `thumbnail` is set (archive-card.html).
