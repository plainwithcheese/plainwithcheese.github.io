{%- comment -%} Every post's published and updated times, shared by the feed (feed.xml) and About's "Last updated" (about.html), so the two always agree. Times are UTC (_config.yml), in the feed's format (2026-11-18T00:00:00+00:00). Sets: pt_posts — the posts (site.posts without drafts), newest first, in the site's own order (same-day posts by filename) pt_pubs — each post's published time, in pt_posts' order: its date, plus a second for every later same-day post, so no two share a time pt_upds — each post's updated time: its `last_modified_at` (the build makes that its latest revision's date), else its published time pt_newest — the latest updated time, "" with no posts. It changes only when a post is added or revised, never with the build's clock. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign pt_posts = site.posts | where_exp: "post", "post.draft != true" -%} {%- assign pt_pubs = "" | split: "" -%} {%- assign pt_upds = "" | split: "" -%} {%- assign pt_newest = "" -%} {%- for pt_post in pt_posts -%} {%- assign pt_i = forloop.index -%} {%- assign pt_day = pt_post.date | date: "%Y-%m-%d" -%} {%- assign pt_bump = 0 -%} {%- for pt_o in pt_posts offset: pt_i -%} {%- assign pt_o_day = pt_o.date | date: "%Y-%m-%d" -%} {%- if pt_o_day == pt_day -%}{%- assign pt_bump = pt_bump | plus: 1 -%}{%- else -%}{%- break -%}{%- endif -%} {%- endfor -%} {%- assign pt_pub = pt_post.date | date_to_xmlschema -%} {%- if pt_bump > 0 -%}{%- assign pt_pub = pt_post.date | date: "%s" | plus: pt_bump | date: "%Y-%m-%dT%H:%M:%S%:z" -%}{%- endif -%} {%- assign pt_upd = pt_pub -%} {%- if pt_post.last_modified_at -%}{%- assign pt_upd = pt_post.last_modified_at | date_to_xmlschema -%}{%- endif -%} {%- assign pt_pubs = pt_pubs | push: pt_pub -%} {%- assign pt_upds = pt_upds | push: pt_upd -%} {%- if pt_upd > pt_newest -%}{%- assign pt_newest = pt_upd -%}{%- endif -%} {%- endfor -%}
