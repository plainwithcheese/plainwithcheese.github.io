{%- comment -%} Sets `sec_code` to the item-code letter(s) for a section slug, from `code:` in _data/categories.yml (default: first letter of its display name, else of the slug). Used to build codes like "03-B". Param: slug — the section slug (a post's `category`) {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign sec_meta = site.data.categories | where: "slug", include.slug | first -%} {%- assign sec_code = sec_meta.code -%} {%- unless sec_code -%}{%- assign sec_code = sec_meta.display | default: include.slug | slice: 0, 1 | upcase -%}{%- endunless -%}
