{%- comment -%} A post's `created:`: when the piece was made. The post's `date` is the day it went up, and everything that sorts by date uses that. Optional: a full date (YYYY-MM-DD) or a year alone (YYYY) when the day isn't known. Sets: created_iso — "2021-04-30" or "2021" ("" when unset) created_str — as Kitchen Facts shows it: "Apr 30, 2021" or "2021" Kitchen Facts shows it as "Created", above "Published", on any post that sets it (any section, e.g. older writing posted later). New work leaves it out: its `date` is the day it goes up. Validates (build FAILS via the missing-include trick): it's a date or a year, and it isn't later than the post's `date`. Param: post {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign created_iso = "" -%} {%- assign created_str = "" -%} {%- if include.post.created -%} {%- assign cd_raw = include.post.created | append: "" | strip -%} {%- assign cd_pub = include.post.date | date: "%Y-%m-%d" -%} {%- if cd_raw.size == 4 -%} {%- assign cd_year = cd_raw | plus: 0 -%} {%- assign cd_back = cd_year | append: "" -%} {%- if cd_back != cd_raw or cd_year < 1000 -%} {%- include errors/CREATED_IS_NOT_A_DATE-write-it-as-YYYY-MM-DD-or-a-year-alone-YYYY.html -%} {%- endif -%} {%- assign cd_pub_year = cd_pub | slice: 0, 4 -%} {%- if cd_raw > cd_pub_year -%} {%- include errors/CREATED_AFTER_PUBLISHED-created-is-later-than-the-post-date.html -%} {%- endif -%} {%- assign created_iso = cd_raw -%} {%- assign created_str = cd_raw -%} {%- else -%} {%- comment -%} A date the filter can't read comes back unchanged, without the "|ok", so this also catches "2021-13-45" or "2021-02-30". {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign cd_check = include.post.created | date: "%Y-%m-%d|ok" -%} {%- assign cd_want = cd_raw | append: "|ok" -%} {%- if cd_raw.size != 10 or cd_check != cd_want -%} {%- include errors/CREATED_IS_NOT_A_DATE-write-it-as-YYYY-MM-DD-or-a-year-alone-YYYY.html -%} {%- endif -%} {%- if cd_raw > cd_pub -%} {%- include errors/CREATED_AFTER_PUBLISHED-created-is-later-than-the-post-date.html -%} {%- endif -%} {%- assign created_iso = cd_raw -%} {%- assign created_str = include.post.created | date: "%b %-d, %Y" -%} {%- endif -%} {%- endif -%}
