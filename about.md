I am plainwithcheese, and this is where I share my creative work.

I post stories, poems, art, and anything else I’ve made that I feel like sharing. Updates come from the kitchen as new pieces are ready.

Everything is free to read, with usage licenses clearly marked per post. Work is made by my hand unless otherwise marked; any AI usage is detailed in the Kitchen Facts for each piece.

This site will never host ads and doesn’t track you. There’s no mailing list, and you won’t find plainwithcheese on social media; any account using the name isn’t me. Use the [RSS feed]({{ '/feed/' | relative_url }}) to hear about new posts.

No veggies, only cheese. Enjoy!
