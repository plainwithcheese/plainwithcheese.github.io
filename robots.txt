# robots.txt
#
# Search engines are allowed: any crawler not named below may crawl
# everything (the last group).
#
# AI crawlers are blocked, both kinds:
#   - training crawlers, which collect pages to train AI models;
#   - AI-answer fetchers, which fetch pages to answer a chatbot or AI search
#     user's question, or to cite in an AI search result.
# Why: to keep this site's pages out of AI training data and out of AI
# tools' answers.
#
# What this can't do:
#   - robots.txt is a request, not a lock. Well-behaved crawlers honor it;
#     some don't.
#   - Google's AI Overviews and Bing's Copilot answers use the search
#     engines' own crawlers (Googlebot, Bingbot), so they can't be blocked
#     here without leaving those search engines.
#
# Link previews (when a link is shared in a chat app or on social media)
# aren't blocked.

# OpenAI: training, ChatGPT search, and pages fetched for ChatGPT users
User-agent: GPTBot
User-agent: OAI-SearchBot
User-agent: ChatGPT-User
Disallow: /
