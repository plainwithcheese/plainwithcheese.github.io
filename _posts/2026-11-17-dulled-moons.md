It’s funny,
How much it burned and tore,
The anguish from before.

Fun agony.
Flesh — discarded clothes on the floor,
After the wolf skin I wore.

But now,
The feeling is no longer there.
Straining, my muscles tear.

Somehow,
This unfolding act of changing
Numbed the painful rearranging.

What once
Snuffed my soul each bright moon
Twisted to near-pleasure soon.

So months
Fly now, my body in tune.
My wolf form, my silver spoon.

I don’t
Think of cures at all,
On stone-still paws, no withdrawal.

I won’t
Remember past nightfall.
Fading still — that paper doll.
